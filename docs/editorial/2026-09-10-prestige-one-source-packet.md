## Historical proposed short update — not release copy

Working headline: **Prestige One sets out AED3bn–4bn Dubai expansion plan**

Format: short-update. Body: 195 words. Source attributions and links should be retained through the news pipeline.

Prestige One Developments plans to invest AED3 billion–4 billion in Dubai during the 2026–2027 property season, according to an Arabian Business report published on 9 September. The proposed spending covers land purchases and new residential and commercial developments.

The report attributes the plans to Hussein Ezz Eddin, the developer’s chief sales officer, speaking at the International Property Show. He said the company had launched four projects since the start of 2026 and was preparing another seven to eight launches during the current season.

Ezz Eddin also described demand for waterfront homes, addresses near Burj Khalifa and residences associated with international hospitality brands. These were the company’s observations about buyer interest.

Before the exhibition, its organiser announced Prestige One’s participation and identified Hilton Residences Dubai Maritime City, FAUCHON Residences, Golf Residences, Berkeley Square and Parkway within its portfolio. That 3 September announcement also described plans to move into retail and commercial development.

IPS ran from 7 to 9 September at Dubai World Trade Centre.

The investment range and additional launches remain announced plans. The report does not give a project-by-project allocation, identify the land purchases or set out construction and handover dates for the proposed programme.
