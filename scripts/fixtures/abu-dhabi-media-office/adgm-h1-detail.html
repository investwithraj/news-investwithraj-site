ADGM reinforces Abu Dhabi’s position as global financial hub

ADGM, the international financial centre of Abu Dhabi, delivered strong growth in the first half of 2026, with assets under management rising 54 per cent year on year.

The number of fund and asset managers based in ADGM reached 190, while the number of funds managed from ADGM rose to 276.
